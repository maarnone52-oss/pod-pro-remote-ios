POD Pro Remote on iPhone and iPad with Web MIDI Browser (free, no paid Apple account)

The panel is published as a web page:
   https://maarnone52-oss.github.io/pod-pro-remote-ios/

INSTALLATION
1. Install "Web MIDI Browser" (free) from the App Store on your iPhone and/or iPad.
2. Connect the MIDI interface to the POD and to the device:
   iPhone with USB-C: USB MIDI interface straight into the port
   iPad Pro Lightning: Apple "Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter"
   Bluetooth: first pair the adapter with the free app "midimittr"
3. Open Web MIDI Browser, type the address above and add it to your bookmarks.
4. At the top of the panel choose "MIDI Out", "MIDI In" and the POD's channel.

LANGUAGE
The panel is in English and Italian and follows the device's language.
To change it tap the IT (or EN) button at the top, next to Compact and Red.
The choice is remembered on that device.

NOTES
- Saved presets stay in the browser on that device: use "Export to file"
  from time to time to keep a copy.

TUNER
The Tuner button, next to Manual, turns the POD's tuner on and off.
The first time, the panel must learn the command from the POD (you need the
cable from the POD's MIDI OUT to the interface's MIDI IN):
1. Tap Tuner in the panel: it changes to "Press Tuner on the POD…".
2. Press Tuner on the POD to turn it on, then press it again to turn it off.
3. From then on the panel's Tuner button controls the tuner.
To learn the command again: press and hold Tuner for about one second.
The learned command is saved on that device (iPhone and iPad each learn it
on their own).

READY-MADE SOUNDS
On the Presets page you will find "Comfortably Numb – Solo" and a softer
variant. Load sends the sound to the POD; Copy to mine adds it to your
presets, so you can edit and overwrite it.
